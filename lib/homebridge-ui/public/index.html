Any Wi-Fi Shelly that measures power or switches: Gen1 to Gen4, also ones paired to Apple Home over Matter, and ones with the HomeKit firmware. Plugs, relays, dimmers, shutter controllers and EM meters. Shellys with a password set can't be read.
